# Landing Page UX Improvements - Role Selection Clarity

## 🎯 Problem Identified
User's son tested the landing page but didn't realize he could choose between Buyer, Seller, or Mechanic roles before scanning.

## ✅ Solutions Implemented

### 1. **Clear Section Header** 📋
Added a prominent "Choose Your Role" header above the role selection cards:
- **Title**: "Choose Your Role" (2xl-3xl font, bold, white)
- **Subtitle**: "Select how you're using this aircraft to get tailored insights"
- **Placement**: Directly above the role cards, impossible to miss

### 2. **Visual Selection Indicator** ✓
Added a "✓ SELECTED" badge to the active role card:
- **Badge**: White background, black text, bold
- **Position**: Top-right corner of selected card
- **Effect**: Makes it crystal clear which role is currently active

### 3. **Existing Premium Features** (Already in place)
- **Card Scaling**: Selected card scales up (105%)
- **Glow Effect**: Selected card has colored glow shadow
- **Ring Border**: Selected card has colored ring
- **Gradient Icon**: Each role has distinct color (Green/Blue/Orange)

## 📊 Before vs After

### Before:
- Role cards were present but not obviously a choice
- Users might scan without realizing they should select a role
- No clear instruction to choose

### After:
- **Prominent header**: "Choose Your Role"
- **Clear instruction**: "Select how you're using this aircraft..."
- **Visual feedback**: "✓ SELECTED" badge on active card
- **Multiple indicators**: Scale, glow, ring, badge all show selection

## 🎨 Visual Hierarchy

1. **Logo & Title** - goTailScan branding
2. **Value Props** - Quick feature highlights
3. **Search Bar** - Enter tail number
4. **👉 "Choose Your Role" Header** - NEW! Makes selection obvious
5. **Role Cards** - Buyer / Seller / Mechanic with features
6. **Authority Badges** - FAA, TC, NTSB, etc.

## 🚀 Impact

**Users will now:**
- ✅ See the "Choose Your Role" instruction immediately
- ✅ Understand they need to select Buyer/Seller/Mechanic
- ✅ Know which role is currently selected (✓ SELECTED badge)
- ✅ Get tailored insights based on their role

**The landing page now guides users through the selection process clearly!**

## 📱 Mobile Friendly

All improvements are responsive:
- Header text scales appropriately (text-2xl md:text-3xl)
- Badge remains visible on mobile
- Cards stack vertically on small screens
- Touch-friendly tap targets
