# Mission Fit: The "Performance Audit" for Buyers

## Core Concept
To explain Mission Fit to a buyer, move the conversation away from "what the plane is" and toward "what the plane does for their life". It is essentially a "Performance Audit" that uses AI to simulate every trip the buyer plans to take, ensuring they aren't buying too much—or too little—airplane for their specific needs.

## The "Elevator Pitch" for Mission Fit
"Most buyers purchase an aircraft based on its maximum specs, but they fly its average mission. Mission Fit analyzes your specific travel patterns—your frequent destinations, passenger loads, and local runway lengths—and gives you a percentage score. It tells you if this tail is the most efficient 'tool' for your actual life, preventing you from overpaying for capabilities you’ll never use."

## How to Break Down the Value (The 3 Pillars)

### 1. Operational Efficiency
*   **How to Explain:** "We compare the aircraft’s fuel burn and cruise speed against your top 5 most frequent routes (e.g., Hamilton to Florida)."
*   **The Check:** "Are you spending $20k extra a year on fuel just to save 10 minutes?"

### 2. Payload Reality Check
*   **How to Explain:** "We calculate if the plane can actually carry your family, luggage, and full fuel safely out of your home airport on a hot day."
*   **The Check:** "This prevents you from buying a 4-seater that can only legally carry 2 people and a suitcase.

### 3. Financial Optimization
*   **How to Explain:** "The AI ranks this tail against others in the same class to see if it’s the 'Best Value' for your specific range requirements."
*   **The Check:** "You might find that a $200k Mooney out-performs a $400k Cirrus for your exact 300-mile mission."

## A Sample "Buyer Mission" Report
If a buyer is looking at a Mooney M20R (like N89RD) in Hamilton, you can show them this:

*   **Primary Mission:** Hamilton (YHM) to Orlando (ORL).
*   **Distance:** ~950 nm.
*   **Mission Fit Score:** 92%.

**The Logic:** "Because the M20R is a 'speed-demon' with low fuel burn, it beats the fleet average for this long-range mission. However, if you plan to carry 4 adults, your Mission Fit drops to 65% due to weight limits. Do you want to prioritize speed or passengers?"

## Why you need this in the $49 "Footprint" Scan
Using Mission Fit as part of your Phase 1 Report creates a powerful "Aha!" moment.

*   **The Strategy:** If the Mission Fit is low (e.g., 40%), the buyer saves $2,000 on a pre-buy inspection by walking away immediately.
*   **The Result:** You just became their hero by saving them thousands for a $49 investment.
